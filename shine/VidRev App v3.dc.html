TypeName / HostValue / Target

On your opt-in page

Every link click adds c2c_tid and UTM parameters (utm_content is the same id) to your opt-in page address. Most form tools store UTM values on their own. To be sure, add a hidden field named c2c_tid to your form and this line to the page:

<script src="https://app.vidrev.app/c2c.js" async></script>

It fills the field, also when the visitor comes back days later.
